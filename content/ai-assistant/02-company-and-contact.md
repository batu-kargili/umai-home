# What UMAI is as a company

UMAI is an **enterprise AI governance company**. It builds the control layer
that organizations need when AI moves out of experiments and into production —
where governance stops being a policy document and becomes an operating
requirement.

The company's premise: once AI reaches real workflows, security, compliance and
risk have to be aligned from day one, not retrofitted after an incident. UMAI's
focus is regulated and controlled environments — organizations that must show
who approved what, what the system was allowed to do, and what actually
happened.

Positioning statement used on the site:

> Building the company foundation for governed AI deployment.

> UMAI is the control layer for enterprise AI teams that need runtime
> guardrails, browser governance, and audit-ready evidence without
> re-architecting the stack.

## What the company optimizes for

- Security, compliance, and risk aligned from day one
- Governance introduced as an operating discipline, not a checkbox
- Built for regulated enterprise adoption
- Trust, oversight, accountability
